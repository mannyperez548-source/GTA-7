# 2.0.0 — Islands & Ambition

- Expanded catalogs to 20 procedural car models and 5 boat types.
- Added 2 planes and a helicopter with arcade flight, altitude controls and airport spawning.
- Added 27 suburban houses, 5 apartments and 3 penthouses; 38 purchasable properties total.
- Enlarged the mainland and added 3 islands, 2 airports and 4 marina/ferry destinations.
- Added 15 legal job roles, timed work, multi-stop tasks, aviation routes and NPC security defense.
- Added 3 fictional robbery contracts, raid/escape stages, personal pursuers, non-graphic stuns, guarding, health, fines, cooldowns, wanted clearance and saved crime reputation.
- Added a Travel menu, location guidance, job dispatch selection and aircraft controls.
- Added stars/moon, improved vehicle geometry, larger ocean and instanced windows.
- Added mocked-transport server regression tests and explicit validation/production limitations.
